HOW TO READ THIS

Added to INDEX:80 means a record first appears in the public Registry. It does not mean the project or person launched on that date. Removed from public index is deliberately neutral and does not mean an entity ceased operating.

The public view is intentionally conservative: additions/removals and low-ambiguity factual fields such as names, official links, policy IDs, established dates and Treasury/Catalyst references. Summary rewrites, tag enrichment, source-list maintenance and relationship housekeeping remain audit-only.
