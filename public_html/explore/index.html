A wallet manages the keys that authorise transactions; your ADA and native assets remain recorded on-chain. The important distinction is who controls the signing keys and how safely recovery credentials are stored.

Staking

Cardano holders can delegate stake to a stake pool while keeping custody of their ADA. Delegation helps determine block-producing stake and can earn protocol rewards; it is not the same as transferring coins to the pool operator.

CExplorer · CardanoScan

Native assets + policy IDs

Tokens and NFTs can exist directly on Cardano's ledger. A policy ID identifies the minting policy; combined with the asset name it lets users distinguish the intended asset from lookalikes with the same ticker or display name.

GOVERNANCE/CARDANO

UNDERSTAND. DELEGATE. PROPOSE. VOTE.

Cardano has on-chain governance for decisions about the network. ADA holders can participate through delegated representatives (DReps), while stake pool operators and the Constitutional Committee also vote on governance actions according to the action type.

ADA holders

ADA holders provide the voting power behind delegated governance. Start here to understand how governance works before delegating to a DRep — without transferring custody of your ADA.

Start with governance ↗

DReps

Delegated representatives receive voting power from ADA holders and vote on governance actions. An ADA holder can also register as a DRep and represent themselves or others.

GovTool · DRepTalk

Stake pool operators

SPOs operate the infrastructure that validates transactions and produces blocks. Under Cardano governance they also vote on specified governance actions, alongside their network-operating role.

CExplorer · PoolTool

Constitutional Committee

The Constitutional Committee assesses whether governance actions are consistent with the Cardano Constitution. Its role is constitutional oversight rather than simply representing delegated stake.

Read the Constitution ↗ · Intersect

Governance actions

Governance actions are on-chain proposals concerning the network. Different action types have different ratification requirements, so the relevant voting bodies depend on what is being proposed.

Action Charts ↗ · CGOV

How to participate

Cardano.org's own guided pathway walks through the three governance roles in order — understand how governance works, delegate your voting power, or stand for a governance role yourself.

ON-CHAIN GOVERNANCE ≠ PROJECT CATALYST

These are related parts of the Cardano ecosystem, but they are not the same system. Cardano's on-chain governance handles governance actions concerning the blockchain and its constitutional governance process. Project Catalyst is Cardano's innovation-funding programme, through which proposals have historically competed for Treasury-backed funding.

INDEX:80's ₳ Treasury Funded view records confirmed historical Catalyst-linked funding evidence for indexed projects. It does not mean those projects have special governance authority, and historical funding is not an endorsement or proof that a project is currently active.
