HOW IT WORKS

THE PUBLISHING PROCESS

Simple by design: one public file, one fingerprint, one manually approved Cardano transaction.

01

→
03

HASH + APPROVE

→
04

CARDANO PROOF

The fingerprint is recorded on Cardano and linked back here.
