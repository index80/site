EDITORIAL NOTE

Disco Solaris is a long-running Cardano-native cyberpunk universe centred on Neo Miami. The project began with its 5,777-citizen NFT collection in February 2022 and has expanded through community storytelling, digital identity and on-chain utilities. Its current official site presents a playable card battle that can incorporate Cardano NFTs into ranked decks.
