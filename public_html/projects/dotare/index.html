EDITORIAL NOTE

Dotare is a Cardano-based universal basic income platform and decentralized endowment. Benefactors pledge ADA, which generates staking and DeFi yield used to support recurring beneficiary distributions; Dotare also operates the DOTAR stake pool and ADA D redemption token.
