EDITORIAL NOTE

Larissa.Health is developing digital infrastructure for maternal health and midwifery, including practitioner verification, self-sovereign identity and AI-governance concepts. Its current roadmap distinguishes completed Web3/stake-pool operations from application, tokenomics and AI components that are still planned or developing. The project operates the Cardano BIRTH stake pool as part of its funding and decentralised-infrastructure model.
