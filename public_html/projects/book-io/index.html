Book.io

MEDIA

Digital publishing and ownership platform for eBooks and audiobooks, allowing authors and publishers to release blockchain-backed editions that readers can own, read, gift and resell. Book.io began on Cardano and now operates as a multichain platform.

EDITORIAL NOTE

Book.io originated in the Cardano ecosystem but is not Cardano-exclusive. Authors and publishers can self-publish eBooks and audiobooks through the platform, and Book.io now operates across multiple chains. Book.io and Stuff.io share the same founding team and digital-library ecosystem, with Stuff.io extending the model into wider digital media. Book.io has publicly disclosed investment/backing from Mark Cuban, Ingram Content Group, BDMI/Bertelsmann and the Hoskinson Family Office.
