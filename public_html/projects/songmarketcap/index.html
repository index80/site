EDITORIAL NOTE

SongMarketCap combines Cardano-native music publishing, community media and token utility. It creates AI-assisted NFT songs and uses Stuff.io's creator portal for minting and sales; its own Whitepaper 2.0 explicitly says this is not a formal partnership. The wider ecosystem includes the $SONG utility token, My Backstage fan/gallery tools, Cardano News and DreamBigSwap, whose current swap provider changed from DexHunter to Gero NEXUS on 16 September 2026.
