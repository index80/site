EDITORIAL NOTE

Stuff.io is the broader digital-media sister platform to Book.io, operated by the same team. Its creator tools support publishing across books, music, video, podcasts and other media. The platform is multichain by design, so INDEX:80 records its continuing Cardano presence without implying Cardano exclusivity. The wider Book.io/Stuff.io ecosystem has attracted backing from Mark Cuban, Ingram Content Group, BDMI/Bertelsmann and the Hoskinson Family Office; the Hoskinson investment announcement specifically names both Stuff.io and Book.io.
