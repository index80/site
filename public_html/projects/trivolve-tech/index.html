EDITORIAL NOTE

Trivolve Tech develops blockchain and AI systems for enterprise and government use. Cardano Foundation reported in June 2026 that Trivolve's Forensic Management System had surpassed 100,000 Cardano mainnet transactions. Its funded IndianChain work with Quixy is integrating Cardano technology into an agriculture-settlement system for Telangana, with the current Catalyst record still showing the project in progress. The Treasury funding shown is linked Catalyst funding for the IndianChain proposal, not a claim that Trivolve Tech as a whole received it.
