# Code of Conduct

INDEX:80 aims to maintain a constructive, technically rigorous and respectful open-source community.

## Expected behaviour

Contributors and maintainers should:

- discuss ideas and disagreements in good faith;
- focus criticism on work, evidence and decisions rather than individuals;
- provide sources where factual corrections are disputed;
- respect privacy and confidentiality;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats or intimidation;
- avoid spam, manipulation and undisclosed conflicts of interest;
- respect the project's editorial-independence rules.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- personal attacks or harassment;
- discriminatory or demeaning language;
- publishing private information without permission;
- deliberately introducing malicious code or data;
- attempts to buy, pressure or manipulate editorial outcomes;
- repeated disruptive behaviour after a maintainer warning.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, close, hide or remove contributions or participation that breach these standards.

Serious concerns should be raised privately with the project maintainer rather than escalated through public argument.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and documented where appropriate.

## Scope

This code applies to INDEX:80 repository participation, issue discussions, pull requests and project-managed community spaces.
