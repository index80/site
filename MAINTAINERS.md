# Maintainers

## Project lead

**Adam Winstanley** — Project Lead, Maintainer and Final Editor. Contact: hello@index80.com

## Authority

The Project Lead has final authority over merges and releases, editorial governance, project inclusion and status decisions, funding applications, maintainer appointments, and licensing decisions. Reviewers and contributors may recommend changes but do not gain editorial or release authority automatically.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers protect repository integrity, review pull requests and issues, keep contributor and security documentation current, preserve editorial independence, and avoid publishing private or internal material.

## Adding maintainers

Future maintainers may be added after sustained, constructive contribution, and will be recorded in this file.
